## Purpose

This document is the brand context for generating polished, editable materials in a Wizeline-inspired style: documents, presentations, one-pagers, internal communications, and any other content. Non-technical teams can upload it as context for an LLM or AI tool so that anything they create stays on brand. It contains guidance only, with no generation templates.

## Audience

Non-technical teams across all functions who use AI tools to create work materials and want their output to follow Wizeline's brand voice, visual identity, and responsible-use principles.

# Brand Foundations

## Brand Essence

Wizeline is a global technology partner focused on performance, AI-accelerated solutions, and transformative outcomes. The brand combines diverse global talent, advanced technology, and outcome-centric delivery.

The visual identity should feel modern, high contrast, confident, human, and technology-forward. The communication style should balance intelligence with approachability: clear thinking, practical examples, and business relevance.

## Mission

> **Mission:** To be the most trusted, outcome-centric partner, using the power of Agentic AI to help the world's top brands scale seamlessly and perform exponentially.

## Core Values

- **Innovation.** Different ways of thinking help interpret and solve customer challenges today while creating the future through continuous learning.
- **Ownership.** A partner-centric mindset drives the evolution of how technology-enabled solutions are developed, designed, and delivered.
- **Community.** Diversity, inclusion, and respect create an environment where people do well and do good.

# Brand Voice

## Voice Overview

Messaging is what the brand says. Tone of voice is how the brand says it. Together they shape how Wizeline sounds in communications. The brand personality pillars are Inclusive, Entrepreneurial, Human, and Results-Driven.

Use language that is respectful, collaborative, practical, grounded in evidence, and focused on outcomes. Avoid vague, inflated, fear-based, dismissive, or overly generic claims.

## Personality Pillars

**Inclusive.** Celebrate diversity and support long-term, continuous culture change. Make every employee feel valued while acknowledging differences and their contribution to culture and business outcomes. Inclusive is empathetic and progressive; it is not fake or restrictive.

> **Follow:** Our Academy offers free classes to communities with access to fewer opportunities to help them learn the most in-demand technical skills.

> **Avoid:** Our Academy offers free classes to help disadvantaged or underserved populations get access to the most in-demand technical skills training.

**Entrepreneurial.** Treat an entrepreneurial mindset as teachable and accessible to everyone. Great innovation is not achieved alone: learn from others, move through mistakes, and find opportunities where others may not. Entrepreneurial is flexible and emotionally intelligent; it is not frenetic or know-it-all.

> **Follow:** I love rapid prototyping because if I fail, I get to fail fast and move on with valuable lessons learned.

> **Avoid:** Perfection is the only acceptable result.

**Human.** Be customer-centric and employee-centric. Treat people with respect and build authentic connections. Human is approachable and naturally curious; it is not fearful or antagonistic.

> **Follow:** Robotic process automation is helping businesses streamline time-consuming manual tasks to give employees the opportunity to work on more valuable projects.

> **Avoid:** Robots are going to take everyone's jobs. Adopt AI now or fall behind.

**Results-Driven.** Focus on getting things done and achieving customer goals. Look for efficient and productive ways to deliver value. Results-Driven is collaborative and adaptable; it is not status quo or inefficient.

> **Follow:** By working with Wizeline, our customer saved over three months of development time versus what they would have accomplished in-house, and they have already generated 100% ROI.

> **Avoid:** Wizeline helped the customer improve their data systems, and we did it in record time.

## Writing Guidance

Write in clear, concise, executive-ready English with short statements that can be scanned quickly, keeping one main idea per section. Use concrete business language: time saved, decisions improved, collaboration strengthened, risk reduced, quality increased, adoption accelerated. Use measurable outcomes only when reliable data is provided or verifiable; do not invent numbers.

Frame AI as a practical accelerator that supports human judgment, and avoid fear-based claims about AI replacing people. Steer away from generic AI phrasing such as unlock the power, revolutionize everything, game-changing, seamless experience, or transform your business without explaining how.

Avoid dense paragraphs: convert long explanations into cards, callouts, labels, short section headers, or numbered steps. Use icons instead of emojis, and do not use emojis anywhere in the material. Avoid hyphen-heavy or dash-heavy phrasing; reach for colons, semicolons, parentheses, numbered steps, labels, cards, callouts, and clear section headers. Prefer polished editorial copy over robotic or generic phrasing.

## Content Principles

The narrative should move from context to value to responsible adoption. Start with a clear reason why the topic matters now, explain benefits through practical work scenarios rather than abstract promises, and connect each section to a business outcome.

Include human accountability: AI accelerates research, synthesis, drafting, and analysis, but humans remain responsible for validation and decisions. Include responsible-use guidance: verify outputs, protect confidential data, check sources, understand assumptions, and escalate sensitive decisions to the right owner.

# Visual System

## Typography

**Space Mono (primary title font).** Large titles, quotes, and short standout statements. Always uppercase. Use sparingly, since it is distinctive and less flexible for long text.

**Nunito Sans (body font).** Body copy, supporting text, captions, notes, labels, small UI-style elements, and most content. For hero titles, use Nunito Sans Bold or Black.

Hierarchy: use Nunito Sans for most text and reserve Space Mono for large, easily scannable titles. Avoid setting long paragraphs in Space Mono, and avoid using too many font weights in one place.

## Logo Usage

The primary digital logo combines the Logomark (the W) and the Wordmark (WIZELINE). For digital and on-screen assets, use the digital logo version. The Wordmark and Logomark can also be used as standalone elements when appropriate.

Use the official logo asset only. Do not redraw, reinterpret, recolor, distort, crop, stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or apply effects to it. Respect the logo safe zone: no text, graphics, or visual elements should appear within the exclusion area around it. Keep the logo small and confident so it identifies the brand without dominating the piece. For presentations, place it on the intro slide, section title slides when needed, and the closing slide; for documents, use a clean header or footer. In all cases, position it top right, bottom right, or in a clean brand bar area with enough breathing room.

## Imagery Style

Imagery should feel candid, authentic, and human, showing real people solving real problems whenever possible. Focus on collaboration, deep work, the human element behind AI, and technology solutions, using authentic workplace scenes, natural interaction, thoughtful concentration, and warm technical environments.

Avoid generic stock photography that feels artificial, staged, robotic, or disconnected from the topic, and avoid generic robot imagery for AI topics. AI-generated imagery may be used only when it feels authentic, realistic, and aligned with the brand. A subtle red light trail, data flow line, or abstract technology signal can work as a visual motif when it supports the message.

## Iconography and Graphic Elements

Use clean icons instead of emojis: simple, modern, editable, and consistent in stroke weight or visual style. Use icons as visual anchors for benefits, steps, responsibilities, use cases, or categories. Do not use decorative icons that distract from the message, and do not mix icon styles in the same piece.

Prefer cards, labels, process lines, steps, diagrams, and callouts over decorative clutter. Use rounded cards and soft containers when grouping related information, and use arrows, connectors, and data-flow lines only when they clarify relationships.

## Layout and Composition

Design with generous whitespace and strong alignment: clean grids, consistent margins, and balanced spacing. Prefer one strong idea per section or page, keep high contrast between text and background, and avoid overcrowding. A layout should feel edited, not filled. For presentations specifically, use a modern 16:9 widescreen format.

Use rounded rectangles, large numbers, compact cards, and structured visual groupings. A simple mental model for structure is title, message, evidence, visual, and takeaway. Use a consistent footer treatment when needed: proprietary and confidential, page number, or a small brand mark.

# AI Content Guidance

## AI Content Style

The tone for AI-related content should be innovative, professional, dynamic, and human-centric. Describe AI as a tool that increases capability, speed, and clarity while preserving human accountability. Do not describe AI as magic or as a replacement for expert judgment, and do not create urgency through fear: use opportunity, evidence, and practical adoption instead.

Use business-oriented phrasing: faster synthesis, stronger research workflows, better first drafts, improved decision support, more consistent documentation, and reduced repetitive work. Include validation as part of the workflow, so facts, figures, sources, links, assumptions, calculations, and business implications are checked by humans.

## Responsible Use Messages

> **Important:** AI accelerates work. Accountability for decisions remains human.

- Always verify facts, figures, sources, links, and assumptions.
- Always apply human judgment to business context, organizational priorities, risk tolerance, and strategic goals.
- Protect confidential data and follow internal information security rules.
- Use AI to draft, structure, summarize, brainstorm, analyze, and accelerate work.
- Do not use AI as the final authority for high-impact business, legal, financial, medical, security, or personnel decisions.

## Quality Checklist

- The material follows the brand palette and maintains high contrast.
- Space Mono appears only in uppercase and only for large titles or standout text.
- Nunito Sans is used for body content and most text.
- The logo is official, unmodified, and has a clear safe zone.
- Icons are used instead of emojis, and there are no emojis anywhere.
- Copy does not rely on hyphen-heavy AI phrasing.
- Writing uses varied structures: colons, semicolons, parentheses, numbered steps, labels, cards, and callouts.
- Each section has one clear idea, and text is concise and scannable.
- No statistics are invented; any data point is provided or verifiable.
